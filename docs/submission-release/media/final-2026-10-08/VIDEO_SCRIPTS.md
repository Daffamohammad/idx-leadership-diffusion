## Walkthrough · three minutes

Where is leadership forming, and how many companies move with it? The Diffusion looks beneath IHSG. Follow a sector reading back to its companies, dates, and calculations.

The Dashboard tracks 132 stocks across 11 IDX sectors, 12 per sector by market capitalization on 2 October 2026. This retrospective set does not cover every listed company.

The main map places 60-day excess return against IHSG on the horizontal axis. The vertical axis is 20-day excess minus 60-day excess. Together, those coordinates show where a sector stands and how its momentum is changing. Leadership uses a separate calculation: 20-day excess and 5-day versus 60-day acceleration. A sector needs at least five eligible contributors before that signal is confirmed.

Use the replay controls to move through 21 daily dates or five weekly dates, from 4 September through 2 October. The underlying price history starts on 9 June, providing the observations needed for the displayed return windows.

Select a sector to inspect its 20-day and 60-day excess returns, rotation phase, participation, and concentration. The equal-weight basket uses the names available for each horizon and compares their prices with IHSG over the same dates.

Coverage varies by stock and horizon. Dividends, splits, rights issues, and other mechanical events remove the affected windows. When an observation is missing, the calculation leaves it out; no missing price is filled. Contributor counts stay beside each reading.

For breadth, the dashboard compares the same names at both dates. That paired count matters: a percentage can move as participation changes, while the displayed denominator shows how many companies were actually comparable.

Coverage and Sources lists all 132 stocks, their latest closes, and the dates covered by their price histories. Those prices support the daily and weekly replay. Year-to-date readings and company-level flow remain tied to the original 66-stock set: 23 names have an action-free return, and company flow spans 5 July to 2 October. No sector reaches the five-name year-to-date threshold.

The other pages combine distinct sources. Market Movers uses the official IDX Stock Summary. Market-level foreign-flow totals come from IDX Daily Statistics, alongside company flow from Sectors API. Broader adjusted-price comparisons use Yahoo Finance data through the yfinance Python client. Ownership uses dated IDX and KSEI disclosures; affiliation alone does not prove legal control.

Methodology lays out the formulas, sources, thresholds, and exclusions. Use it to understand what each reading can support, then follow a sector back to its constituents. That is the point: a clearer view of leadership beneath the index.
